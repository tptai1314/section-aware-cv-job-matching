\begin{abstract}
Bidirectional matching between candidate resumes (CVs) and job descriptions (JDs) is fundamental to online recruitment and freelance marketplaces. Conventional pipelines rely on lexical matching or compress an entire document into a single dense vector, which dilutes rare technical skills in long resumes and suffers from transformer context truncation ($256$--$512$ tokens), dropping project and experience histories located in the second half of CVs. Furthermore, practical hiring requires asymmetric skill coverage (distinguishing mandatory from preferred requirements) and transparent decision auditability that black-box neural rankers fail to provide. We present a CPU-efficient, bidirectional architecture combining \textbf{Section-Aware Multi-Vector Representation}, an upgraded \textbf{3-Tier Skill Taxonomy} (incorporating heading/clause-aware requirement parsing, negation guarding, multi-hop relational traversal, semantic fallback, and dynamic boundary renormalization), and a \textbf{Calibrated Hybrid Ranking Engine}. Each document is segmented into up to four functional sections (\textit{skills}, \textit{experience}, \textit{education}, \textit{summary}), encoded independently, and aligned via cross-section pairing with dynamic weight renormalization. We further examine leakage-free supervised extensions via Section-Aware CoSENT bi-encoder fine-tuning and monotonicity-constrained Learning-to-Rank (LTR). Extensive experiments across four public benchmarks---\textsc{resume-job-description-fit} ($1{,}759$ test pairs across $71$ JDs; $76$ clean held-out train JDs), high-density \textsc{med2425} ($8{,}575$ pairs across $41$ JDs and $277$ CVs), \textsc{job\_resume\_fit}, and \textsc{resume-score-details}---yield three core findings: (1)~section-aware representation mitigates truncation on long resumes ($\Delta\text{nDCG@10} = +0.0311$ for CVs $>600$ words) and significantly improves reverse job recommendation ($\text{CV} \rightarrow \text{JD}$, $+0.0185$ nDCG@10, $p = 0.0022$); (2)~Calibrated Hybrid achieves the highest training-free nDCG@10 ($0.6196$ Setting~A, $0.7212$ Setting~B), outperforming a Cross-Encoder significantly after 15-test Holm--Bonferroni correction ($\bar{\Delta} = +0.0666$, $p_{\text{adj}} = 0.0442$) at $2.69$\,ms ranking latency ($33.44$\,ms online vs.\ $1{,}178.05$\,ms), and delivers $+0.0856$ to $+0.0860$ nDCG@10 gains in high-density bidirectional \textsc{med2425} screening ($p < 0.05$); and (3)~leakage-free CoSENT fine-tuning combined with Monotonic Linear LTR adds another $+0.0439$ nDCG@10 ($p = 0.0020$) at $0.0012$\,ms CPU overhead.

\end{abstract}
