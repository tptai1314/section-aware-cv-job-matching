\section{Experimental Setup and Evaluation Protocol}

\subsection{Datasets and Leakage-Free Splits}
We evaluate across four public benchmarks spanning both in-domain screening and zero-shot cross-dataset transfer:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Primary Test Split} (\texttt{cnamuangtoun/resume-job-description-fit})~\cite{huggingface_resume_fit}: $1{,}759$ labeled pairs across $71$ unique JDs and $477$ CVs spanning IT and business domains, labeled on a $3$-level graded scale: Grade~$0$ (\textit{No Fit}, $857$ pairs, $48.7\%$), Grade~$1$ (\textit{Potential Fit}, $444$ pairs, $25.2\%$), and Grade~$2$ (\textit{Good Fit}, $458$ pairs, $26.1\%$).
    \item \textbf{Held-Out Clean Train Split}: From the $280$ JDs in \texttt{train.csv}, $204$ JDs ($72.9\%$) were inspected during rule development or sensitivity sweeps. Per our pre-registered plan (commit \texttt{d22eb66}), we isolate the remaining $76$ untouched JDs ($1{,}829$ pairs, $547$ CVs) to audit out-of-sample generalization.
    \item \textbf{High-Density Bidirectional Split} (\texttt{med2425/\allowbreak resume-job-fit-\allowbreak merged-v1}): Filtering for IT domains (software, data, AI) yields $8{,}575$ pairs across $41$ JDs and $277$ CVs ($\approx 209$ CVs/JD), annotated by Qwen2.5-32B at temperature $0$. We evaluate both $\text{JD}\rightarrow\text{CV}$ ($N=41$) and $\text{CV}\rightarrow\text{JD}$ ($N=272$ valid queries with $\ge 5$ JDs and $\ge 1$ positive). For zero-shot transfer of supervised models, we also exclude all CVs overlapping with \texttt{train.csv} ($7{,}418$ strictly unseen pairs, $240$ CVs).
    \item \textbf{External Zero-Shot Benchmarks}: \texttt{job\_resume\_fit} ($23$ JDs, $2{,}385$ pairs, $0\%$ overlap, continuous scores $0$--$100$) and \texttt{resume-score-details} ($24$ ranking JDs, $851$ pairs, $0\%$ overlap, GPT-4o rubric scores).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Evaluated Models, Metrics, and Statistical Protocol}
We evaluate $14$ training-free configurations: Random Floor ($100$ permutations), Oracle Ceiling, BM25/TF-IDF~\cite{robertson2009probabilistic}, MiniLM Single-Vector Flat ($256$ tokens), Chunk-Mean ($128$-word windows, stride $64$), Chunk-MaxSim (ColBERT-style~\cite{khattab2020colbert}), BGE-M3 Flat and Section-Aware ($8{,}192$ tokens)~\cite{chen2024bge}, MiniLM Section-Aware variants (Pure, Fallback, Mixed $0.65/0.35$), Dense+BM25 ($L_0$), $2\times 3$ Taxonomy ablations, Calibrated Hybrid ($\alpha=\beta=0.60$), RRF+Taxonomy ($k=60$)~\cite{cormack2009reciprocal}, Full Cross-Encoder (\texttt{ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2}), and Cascaded 2-Stage (Top-$10$/Top-$20$). For supervised comparisons, \texttt{minilm-cvjd-ft} is trained on $230$ clean train JDs ($50$ validation JDs for early stopping, excluding all $68$ test-overlapping CVs), and LTR rankers are fitted on out-of-sample validation queries to prevent stacking leakage.

Following TREC/SIGIR standards, we report $\text{nDCG}@K$ ($K\in\{5,10\}$), $\text{MRR}$, $\text{MAP}$, $\text{P}@5$ at threshold $\tau=1$ ($\text{rel}\ge 1$), and Macro-Spearman $\rho$ (tied midranks) averaged over $N_{\text{shuffles}}=50$ tie-breaking permutations ($z\sim\text{Uniform}(-10^{-9},10^{-9})$) across \textbf{Setting~A} (all $71$ queries) and \textbf{Setting~B} ($61$ filtered queries with $\text{rel}\ge 1$). Across $L=15$ primary hypothesis comparisons, we report paired $t$-tests, Wilcoxon tests, $10{,}000$-resample bootstrap $95\%$ CIs, and \textbf{Holm--Bonferroni} adjusted $p$-values ($\alpha_{\text{stat}}=0.05$). All CPU latencies are measured on an Intel Core i7 CPU (PyTorch CPU, $10$ warm-ups, $15$--$20$ repetitions) decomposed into $T_{\text{index}}$, $T_{\text{query}}$, $T_{\text{rank}}$ ($N=25$), and $T_{\text{online}} = T_{\text{query}} + T_{\text{rank}}$.
