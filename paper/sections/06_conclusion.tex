\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

This paper presented a training-free, CPU-efficient architecture for bidirectional CV--Job matching that integrates Section-Aware Multi-Vector representations with a Calibrated Hybrid ranking engine and a 3-tier skill ontology. By segmenting documents into functional subspaces and applying cross-section alignment with dynamic weight renormalization, our approach eliminates context truncation on over $95\%$ of candidate profiles while providing traceable section- and skill-level justifications.

Comprehensive empirical evaluation across two public international benchmarks---\textsc{resume-job-description-fit} ($1,759$ test pairs, $71$ JDs; plus $76$ clean held-out train queries) and the high-density \textsc{med2425} dataset ($8,575$ pairs across $41$ JDs and $277$ CVs)---yields three principal scientific takeaways:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Truncation Mitigation and Length-Stratified Gains}: While Section-Aware Dense achieves statistical parity with Flat Single-Vector on pooled short-pool queries, stratifying by document length reveals a monotonic trajectory ($\Delta\text{nDCG@10}$ rising from $-0.0112$ on short resumes $\le 350$ words to $+0.0126$ on $>350$ words and $\mathbf{+0.0239}$ on long resumes $>600$ words). Furthermore, in reverse job recommendation ($\text{CV} \rightarrow \text{JD}$, $N=272$ queries), Section-Aware Mixed significantly outperforms Flat Single-Vector ($\bar{\Delta} = +0.0185$ nDCG@10, $t=3.09, p=0.0022$).
    \item \textbf{Decisive Value of Structured Ontology in High-Density Bidirectional Pools}: In small candidate pools ($N=25$), ontology rules primarily provide structural explainability and prerequisite auditing. However, in high-density competitive pools ($\approx 209$ CVs/JD on \textsc{med2425}), Calibrated Hybrid delivers statistically significant improvements over Single-Vector in both candidate screening ($\text{JD} \rightarrow \text{CV}$: $\bar{\Delta} = \mathbf{+0.0849}$ nDCG@10, $+15.7\%$, $p=0.0211$) and job recommendation ($\text{CV} \rightarrow \text{JD}$: $\bar{\Delta} = \mathbf{+0.0855}$ nDCG@10, $+15.4\%$, $p<0.0001$), as well as over Dense+BM25 ($p=0.0457$).
    \item \textbf{Pareto-Optimal CPU Efficiency vs.\ Neural Cross-Encoders}: On commodity CPU hardware ($N=25$), Calibrated Hybrid scores candidates in $T_{\text{rank}} = 2.69$\,ms ($T_{\text{online}} = 33.44$\,ms end-to-end including query encoding), significantly outperforming zero-shot Cross-Encoder reranking ($\bar{\Delta} = +0.0632$ nDCG@10, $p_{\text{RQ}} = 0.0082$) at $35\times$ lower latency ($1,178.05$\,ms).
\end{enumerate}

In future work, we plan to integrate visual document layout analysis for complex multi-column graphic PDFs, expand our skill ontology via open occupational standards (ESCO/O*NET) to cover non-IT disciplines, and conduct human recruiter user studies to quantify practical screening time savings.
