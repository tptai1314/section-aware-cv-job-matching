\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

In this work, we investigated a section-aware multi-vector retrieval and hybrid ranking architecture for bidirectional CV--Job recommendation. By decomposing documents into functional sections with dynamic weight renormalization, the multi-vector representation isolates structured competencies while preserving full-document context. Across extensive experiments on the public benchmark \textsc{resume-job-description-fit}, our Calibrated Hybrid pipeline attains the highest observed MAP of $0.6366$ on all queries ($0.7410$ on filtered positive queries) and consistent rank correlation ($\rho_{\text{mac}} = 0.2954$). However, rigorous multiplicity-controlled testing reveals that performance differences among top neural bi-encoder configurations remain within bootstrap confidence intervals encompassing zero. Consequently, Single-Vector Dense represents the most computationally economical configuration ($T_{\text{online}} \approx 19.5$\,ms, $T_{\text{rank}} < 0.10$\,ms for $N=25$) for latency-critical production environments, while section-aware hybrid modeling provides structured capability for enforcing recruiter prerequisite constraints. Future work will investigate learned section weights on validated training sets and user studies evaluating recruiter interpretability.
