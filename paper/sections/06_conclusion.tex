\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

We presented a CPU-efficient bidirectional CV--Job matching framework combining \textbf{Section-Aware Multi-Vector Representation} with dynamic weight renormalization, an upgraded \textbf{3-Tier Skill Taxonomy} (clause/heading-aware classification, negation guarding, multi-hop graph traversal, semantic fallback, and boundary renormalization), and \textbf{Calibrated Hybrid Ranking}, complemented by leakage-free CoSENT fine-tuning and Monotonic LTR extensions. Across four public benchmarks, our experiments demonstrate: (1)~monotonic truncation mitigation on long resumes ($+0.0311$ $\text{nDCG}@10$ for CVs $>600$ words) and significant reverse-direction gains ($\text{CV}\rightarrow\text{JD}$, $+0.0185$, $p=0.0022$); (2)~top training-free accuracy ($0.6196$ Setting~A, $0.7212$ Setting~B) and large high-density gains on \textsc{med2425} ($+0.0856$ to $+0.0860$ $\text{nDCG}@10$, $p<0.05$); and (3)~CPU Pareto optimality ($T_{\text{rank}}=2.69$\,ms, $T_{\text{online}}=33.44$\,ms), significantly outperforming Cross-Encoder reranking after 15-test Holm--Bonferroni correction ($\bar{\Delta}=+0.0666$, $p_{\text{adj}}=0.0442$) at $35.2\times$ faster speed, while CoSENT fine-tuning plus Monotonic Linear LTR adds another $+0.0439$ $\text{nDCG}@10$ ($p=0.0020$) at $0.0012$\,ms overhead. Future work includes vision layout analysis for graphical PDFs, ESCO/O*NET alignment, and live recruiter user studies.
