\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

In this work, we introduced a production-viable section-aware multi-vector retrieval and hybrid ranking architecture for bidirectional CV--Job recommendation. By decomposing documents into functional sections with dynamic weight renormalization, our system overcomes the semantic dilution inherent in flat single-vector bi-encoders. When integrated with a 3-tier skill taxonomy supporting hierarchical relationships and asymmetric requirement coverage, our Calibrated Hybrid pipeline achieves a Mean Average Precision of $0.6366$ on all queries ($0.7410$ on filtered positive queries) and consistent rank correlation ($\rho_{\text{mac}} = 0.2954$, pooled $\rho_{\text{mic}} = 0.1944$) on the international benchmark \textsc{resume-job-description-fit}. Crucially, our architecture operates with candidate ranking latency under $3.48$\,ms per query ($N=25$) on commodity multi-core CPUs---substantially faster than deep Cross-Encoder rerankers ($1,174.67$\,ms) while exhibiting stable domain performance and mitigating token truncation penalties. Future work will investigate learned section weights and multi-modal document layout modeling.
